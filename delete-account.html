Delete your Collar account

In the app

  1. Open the menu (☰) on the map.
  2. Choose Settings, then Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.

It happens at once and cannot be undone.

Without the app

Email support@collar.co.il from any address with the phone number of the account. We will confirm it is yours with a code sent to that number, and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what stays

  • Deleted: your profile, photo, date of birth, conversations and the files in them, notification settings and device tokens.
  • Businesses: the business is closed and its posts come off the map. Conversations that candidates started with it stay readable to those candidates.
  • Kept: if the account was banned, a one-way fingerprint of the phone number, so it cannot sign up again. Usage events are kept up to 180 days and are then deleted.
