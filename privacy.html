Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what personal information the Collar app and this site collect, why, who handles it for us, how long we keep it, and what you can ask of us. We wrote it in plain language. If anything is unclear, write to support@collar.co.il.

Who we are

Collar is operated by Ori Monrov, a sole proprietor registered in Israel (business no. [to be added]), who is responsible for the database described here. Contact: support@collar.co.il.

What we collect

How we use it

  • To run the marketplace: show jobs, deliver messages, files and notifications.
  • To review job posts automatically before they appear, for spam and rule-breaking, and to translate them into the languages the app supports.
  • To prevent abuse: limits on how often things can be done, reports, blocks and suspensions. If an account is banned we keep a one-way fingerprint (hash) of its phone number, even after deletion, so the same number cannot sign up again.
  • To answer you, fix problems and improve the app.
  • When the law requires it.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who handles it for us

These services process information on our behalf, only for the purposes above:

  • Supabase: our database, file storage and servers (currently located in Singapore).
  • Google: push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging), app integrity checks (Firebase App Check), maps and address search (Google Maps Platform), and AI models (Gemini) that review and translate job posts and power search.
  • Inforu: sending the SMS sign-in codes (Israel).
  • Apple and Google app stores: payments, when the app offers paid features, together with RevenueCat, which confirms purchases.

Some of these services store information outside Israel. We use them under contracts and safeguards consistent with Israeli rules on transferring data abroad.

Messages you choose to translate inside a conversation are translated on your own phone; they are not sent anywhere for that.

Other people see what the app shows them: your profile to those you message, and job posts to everyone.

How long we keep it

  • Your account information: until you delete your account.
  • When you delete your account: your profile, photo, conversations and files are deleted. A business's account is closed, and the conversations candidates started with it stay readable to those candidates.
  • Deleted job posts: removed for good after 30 days.
  • Usage events: 180 days. Cached searches and address lookups: 30 days.
  • The phone-number fingerprint of a banned account: as long as the ban lasts.

Your rights

Under Israel's Protection of Privacy Law you may ask to see the information we hold about you, and to correct or delete it. You can edit your profile and delete your account in the app (Menu → Settings → Delete account), or write to us. We answer within 30 days.

Security

Information travels encrypted. Each person can reach only their own data and the conversations they are part of; files sent in conversations are private to them. No system is perfectly safe, so tell us at once if you notice something wrong.

Age

Anyone may browse jobs. Applying for a job requires being at least 16. Collar is not meant for children under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will say so in the app before the change takes effect.
