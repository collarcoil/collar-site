What Collar is

Rules for job posts

  • Post real, current jobs that you are hiring for.
  • Pay at least the minimum wage required by Israeli law, and state the pay honestly.
  • Do not discriminate. Under the Equal Employment Opportunities Law, a post may not exclude people for their gender, age, religion, nationality, origin, disability, parenthood, sexual orientation or similar, unless the law allows it for that job.
  • Never charge a person looking for work for applying or for getting the job.
  • Follow employment law, including the Youth Employment Law when hiring people under 18.

Posts are reviewed, partly automatically. We may refuse, pause or remove a post that breaks these rules, and suspend the account behind it.
