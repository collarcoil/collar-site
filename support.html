Support

Write to support@collar.co.il. We usually answer within two working days.

Common questions

I didn't get the sign-in code

Check the number and the country code, wait a minute, then ask for a new code. Codes can only be requested every so often, to stop abuse.

Someone is bothering me

Open the conversation, tap the menu at the top, and report or block them. Blocked people cannot write to you.

A job post looks wrong

Open the job and report it. We review reports and remove posts that break the rules.

Too many notifications

Menu → Settings → Notifications lets you choose what to hear about, set quiet hours, or mute a single conversation or job.
