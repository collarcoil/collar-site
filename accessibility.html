Accessibility statement

Last updated: October 2026

We want everyone to be able to find work and hire on Collar, including people with disabilities.

The app

  • Works with the phone's screen reader (TalkBack on Android, VoiceOver on iPhone): buttons and controls have spoken names, including whether your message was sent or seen.
  • Follows the phone's text size, and works in light and dark mode.
  • Supports right-to-left languages, and translates job posts and messages between languages.

This site

This site aims to meet Israeli Standard 5568 (based on WCAG 2.0, level AA): readable contrast, keyboard navigation with visible focus, a structure screen readers can follow, and text that can be enlarged.

Known limits

The map itself is visual. If it gets in your way, write to us and we will help you find jobs near you.

Tell us about a problem

Accessibility contact: Ori Monrov, support@collar.co.il. Tell us what you were trying to do and on which device. We will answer and fix what we can.
